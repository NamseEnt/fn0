This addendum supersedes the provisional comparative_valid_runs=0 statement in METHODOLOGY-CLASSIFICATION.txt for the reduced quick-comparison scope.
The reduced comparison accepts completed runs when all three engines for the same condition and repetition have identical actual seeds. The selected OCI data contains 12 such runs: 9 core-suite records at repetition 1 for Point Get c16, distributed Query16 c16, and pure write c16 width 1; and 3 smoke-suite records for mixed 50/50 c16 width 1.
The core suite used 2,000 ms warmup and 10,000 ms measurement; the smoke suite used 500 ms warmup and 1,500 ms measurement. These are recorded as reused runs at their actual durations, not as 1,000/5,000 ms runs.
The interrupted wider-suite attempt remains preserved. Its seed differed from other repetition numbers, which is irrelevant under the reduced same-condition/same-repetition comparison rule. The attempt itself exited 143 after interruption and is not part of the selected 12 runs.
