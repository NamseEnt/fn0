# Final Comparison Audit

## Scope and status

This audit links the current implementation and preserved evidence to the six-phase plan. The 111-case 30-second matrix completed at source `ee6cd94f1a4e7b2bab7af603bc8983bbf8184542`, including the separately budgeted D capacity sample and standard D repetitions. The approved representative five-minute confirmation completed its selected 21 A/E cases; the other 90 original definitions were outside that selection. A separate seven-path memory-only diagnostic completed at source `843f0ea1598a4fa87284d1e3ee421612f288b4c2` and is not performance evidence. A bounded read-inclusive history checker passed its four supported configurations; arbitrary histories remain outside its finite proof scope. The memory-exceed axis remains deferred by user. RocksDB condition-key locking remains opt-in, with Exclusive as the default. The six approved phases are complete within this evidence scope; limitations and deferred work are stated separately below.

## Evidence limits and deferred scope

The RocksDB Get check validates the encoded record length, protocol value-size limit, and response-frame budget before copying the present payload into the Rust result vector. The native read buffer remains allocated. Query and Scan check cumulative budget before copying each result value, after key decoding/native iterator access, and earlier result documents may already exist. The conservative B-tree/B-link Get frame estimate remains unchanged; this audit does not claim a universal pre-materialization guard across all APIs and backends.

The memory diagnostic provides the approved parent/backend process split and concurrent group peaks. Backend RSS includes the adapter/runtime/transport, so it is not native-only allocator RSS. The seven second-scan results do not retroactively prove residency during the earlier five-minute runs or establish a general effective-cache-to-working-set ratio. Memory-exceed experiments remain Deferred by user.

The other 90 five-minute definitions were outside the approved representative selection. Arbitrary histories are outside the finite checker’s proof scope. These are scope limits, not remaining required runs. Preserved failed attempts and their evidence remain available. Process-crash and power-loss claims remain distinct. No required work remains within the approved six-phase scope.
