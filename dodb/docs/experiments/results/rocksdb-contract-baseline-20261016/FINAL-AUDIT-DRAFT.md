# Final Comparison Audit Draft

## Scope and status

This draft links the current implementation and preserved evidence to the six-phase plan. It is not a final acceptance report: the 111-case 30-second matrix has completed at source `ee6cd94f1a4e7b2bab7af603bc8983bbf8184542`, including the separately budgeted D capacity sample and standard D repetitions. The approved representative five-minute confirmation completed 21 of the original 111 definitions; the other 90 are outside the selected five-minute subset. A bounded read-inclusive history checker passed its four supported configurations, with scope limits recorded below; this is not a proof for arbitrary histories. The memory-exceed axis remains deferred by user. RocksDB condition-key locking remains opt-in, with Exclusive as the default.

## Remaining work

1. Preserve the interrupted original A/r2 case and its database separately from the successful recovery root; its incomplete measurement was not reused as a completed case.
2. Keep the full 111-case five-minute definition distinct from the selected 21-case A/E representative subset. The remaining 90 cases were not run as five-minute cases.
3. Record the unmet response-budget criterion precisely: Get copies one value before the wrapper budget check. Query and Scan check the cumulative budget before each result-value copy, but after key decoding and native iterator access. Do not call this universal pre-materialization enforcement.
4. Record that RSS was not split into harness and native-engine components. Collector vector capacities, process measurement-window RSS, whole-child maximum RSS, and RocksDB properties are separate observations, not an engine RSS decomposition.
5. Record that effective cache capacity and the resident working-set/cache ratio were not measured. The memory-exceed axis remains deferred by user; no OOM, over-cache, or page-residency claim is supported.
6. Finish the six-phase audit against linked evidence. Named API, correctness, history, health, and selected comparison gates passed, but the broad response-budget, memory/cache residency, unselected long-run definitions, and arbitrary-history requirements remain incomplete. Keep process-crash and power-loss claims distinct.
