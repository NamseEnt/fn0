## Outcome interpretation

Classify correctness, operational validity, SLO, resource acceptance, and performance separately for every cell. Correctness uses the post-measurement and reopen full-population oracles. Operational validity requires clean source and binary identity, matching seed and ordered-request hashes across paired routes, complete reports, and no remaining workload processes. SLO acceptance requires every five-minute measurement, checkpoint-extension, and recovery window to pass. Resource acceptance requires the fixed cgroup limits and application caps, the checkpoint-cycle and long-reader gates, no cgroup limit events, the 32 GiB allocated-data ceiling, and no increase in B-link checkpoint debt during the final four measurement windows. Performance reporting includes completed requests per second, the worst five-minute read/write p99 bounds, measurement/recovery cgroup peaks, allocated database bytes, and cache/device-read coverage; bounded observations are not engine maxima.

The demand-paged candidate is adoptable only for a tested condition where its correctness, operational, SLO, and resource gates pass, it has a higher accepted active-tenant frontier than the shared-resource TransactionDB comparator, and three-repetition paired results at common accepted populations show a meaningful peak-memory and allocated-storage advantage without losing the frozen SLO. A valid measured failure or lack of that advantage supports non-adoption for the tested condition. Use evidence insufficient only when source/input pairing or required execution evidence is invalid or absent without a measured acceptance failure. Screening repetitions determine whether the matrix continues; they do not count as comparative performance evidence.
