## Capacity and execution gate

The prior 266 GiB gate incorrectly multiplied setup and checkpoint overlap as separate allocations although both are inside each backend's global disk cap, and it assumed parallel backend setup although all cells are sequential. The corrected peak model permits one active backend only: 32 GiB maximum database plus checkpoint/scratch overlap under that backend's global disk cap, 5 GiB for reports/logs/manifests and one preserved failed cell, and the mandatory 10 GiB ZFS reserve. Require at least 47 GiB free before launch and before each new cell. Stop the entire matrix after the first failed cell, preserve that cell, and launch no additional cell if free space would cross the reserve. Successful cell databases may be removed after reports, hashes, and oracles are preserved. The latest free-space reading is recorded in the T17 execution receipt after the final build cache is reclaimed.
